# Attribution

This repository is an internal workshop demo. It translates, voices and dubs openly licensed English
content into Hindi and Telugu with Sarvam AI APIs. **All translations, synthetic voices and dubs are
derivative works generated by machine for a workshop demo. They are not official translations.**

## Pepper&Carrot (comic)

- **Work:** *Pepper&Carrot*, Episode 12 "Autumn Clearout", pages 1–4
- **Author:** David Revoy, https://www.peppercarrot.com
- **License:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- **Sources used:**
  - Text and speech-bubble SVGs: https://framagit.org/peppercarrot/webcomics/-/tree/master/ep12_Autumn-Clearout
  - Artwork and English renders: https://www.peppercarrot.com/0_sources/ep12_Autumn-Clearout/
- **Changes:** the artwork is not modified. The original English text is replaced on screen by Hindi and Telugu
  machine translations (Sarvam Mayura) layered over the original speech bubbles, and each bubble has synthetic
  speech (Sarvam Bulbul). The original English pages are shown unchanged next to them.
